Blog Keyword Research

Last updated: 2026-09-30

Blog Keyword Research is a private, internal tool used by a single blog operator to choose topics for tutorial articles.

What it does

  • Uses the Google Ads API (Keyword Planning) to read keyword ideas and average monthly search volumes.
  • Uses the results only to decide which tutorial topics to write.

What it does not do

  • It does not create, edit, or manage ads, campaigns, or billing.
  • It does not access any other person's Google account. Only the operator signs in, with their own account.
  • It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect, sell, or share personal information.
